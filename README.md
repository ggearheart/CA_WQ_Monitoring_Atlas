# California Water Quality Monitoring Atlas

A static, multi-plate atlas of statewide ambient surface water quality monitoring in California, centered on SB 1070 (2006, the California Water Quality Monitoring Council) and AB 982 (1999, the Surface Water Ambient Monitoring Program).

- `index.html` and `plates/` are the pages. No build step; serve the folder with any static server.
- `assets/refs.js` holds every reference. Cite in a plate with `<a data-ref="key"></a>`.
- `sources/` holds copies of the primary documents the statute and budget plates were checked against.

Draft. Not an official State publication. Plate IV (Budget) has open figures to confirm before sharing.
