Waste Discharge Permit Fund Information – Fund Growth

This document provides a historical overview of the Waste Discharge Permit Fund
(WDPF), including the fund’s growth, the growth of each water quality program, and a
summary of budget augmentations and foundational shifts. It also includes Water
Quality (WQ) position allocation and expenditure data.

Chart 1: WDPF Fee Fund Growth Fiscal Year (FY) 2009-10 to FY 2025-26
Chart 1 shows the growth of the WDPF from FY 2009-10 to FY 2025-26.

The bar chart represents the WDPF in FY 2009-10 at approximately $76 million. One
thing to point out is during this time, the Water Quality program was not only being
supported by WDPF but also by GF of around $21 million. The GF was supporting our
Foundational programs, TMDL, Basin Planning Programs and monitoring programs.

The total funding for the WQ program in FY 2009-10 was approximately $100 million.

In addition, Chart 1 shows the primary sources driving the growth of the fund from
$76 million in FY 2009-10 to $197 million in FY 2025-26. The $76 million in FY 2009-10
with standard annual increases of about 3.864%, equates to approximately $112 million
in FY 2025-26. Augmentations to the WDPF since FY 2009-10 are grouped into three
sources:

   ·   Foundation Shift – Includes general fund shifts for Basin Planning and TMDL
       and federal funds shifts for ambient monitoring, to fee authority.

   ·   General Augmentation – Includes new funding for activity that is not specific to
       any single fee-paying program within Water Quality and costs are shared across
       the fee-paying programs.

   ·   Program Augmentation – New funding for activity specific to a single fee-paying
       program.
