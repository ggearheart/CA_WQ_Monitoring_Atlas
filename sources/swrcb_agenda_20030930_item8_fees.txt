BOARD MEETING SESSION
SEPTEMBER 30, 2003

ITEM  8

SUBJECT

SUBJECT:  PROPOSED REVISIONS TO THE STATE WATER RESOURCES CONTROL BOARD’S ANNUAL FEE SCHEDULE

DISCUSSION

The fiscal year 2003-04 State Budget shifts the remaining General Fund support of the water quality regulatory programs to fees.  As appropriated in the Governor’s Budget, funding from fees would increase by $16 million, while General Fund support would be reduced by a like amount for regulatory activities.

Legislation recently passed by the Legislature and signed by the Governor (ABX1 10, Oropeza) eliminates the statutory cap on the SWRCB fees.  This legislation also 1) eliminates the exemption from annual fees for dairies and other confined animal feedlots and requires the SWRCB to consider specific criteria in establishing annual fees for this type of discharge; 2) authorizes the SWRCB to establish a fee sufficient to recover the costs associated with processing “no exposure” certifications issued in accordance with a general industrial storm water permit; and 3) designates costs that can be recovered through fees to include conducting surface water and groundwater monitoring and modeling.

The staff recommendation includes proposed fee schedules for dairies and confined animal feeding operations and for “no exposure” certification.  The staff recommendation reflects an additional $6.8 million in fee revenue to be generated to cover the cost of the ambient monitoring program, pursuant to the final Budget Act as signed by the Governor.  The ambient monitoring program costs will be assessed as a surcharge to all fee schedules and will be applied prior to any other surcharges.  Waste Discharge Requirements, Discharge to Landfills and Confined Animal Feeding Operations will be assessed a 9% surcharge and NPDES programs an 18.5% surcharge.
 
POLICY ISSUE

Should the SWRCB adopt through emergency regulations, the revised terms of Water Code Sections 13260 and the provisions of Water Code Section 13260.2, which would not take effect until October 28, 2003. The revised fee schedule will be applied to all fees due and payable for fiscal year 2003-2004.

FISCAL IMPACT

The Budget act for fiscal year 2003/04 increased fee revenues needed for the core regulatory program to $54.8 million.  All fee levels must be increased to generate the fee revenue appropriated in the State Budget Act.
RWQCB IMPACT
Yes, all regions

STAFF RECOMMENDATION

That SWRCB: 

Adopts emergency regulations approving the revisions to Title 23, Division 3, Chapter 9, Article 1, Section 2200 and adding Section 2200.3 of the California Code of Regulations, as proposed by staff.

STATE WATER RESOURCES CONTROL BOARD
RESOLUTION NO. 2003-

ADOPTING EMERGENCY REGULATION REVISIONS TO THE FEE SCHEDULES CONTAINED IN TITLE 23, DIVISION 3, CHAPTER 9, ARTICLE 1, SECTION 2200 OF THE CALIFORNIA CODE OF REGULATIONS

WHEREAS:

The 2003 State Budget Act requires that the entire general fund portion of the SWRCB’s Core Regulatory Program be paid by those to whom waste discharge requirements are issued.
The 2003 State Budget Act appropriates an additional $6.8 million in fee revenue to fund the SWRCB’s monitoring program, to be paid by those to whom waste discharge requirements are issued.
Water Code Section 13260 requires each person for whom waste discharge requirements are issued to pay an annual fee to the SWRCB.
Water Code Section 13260 requires the SWRCB to adopt a schedule of fees by emergency regulation.
The fee schedule for waste discharge requirements adopted pursuant to Water Code Section 13260 is found at Title 23, Division 3, Chapter 9, Article 1, Section 2200 of the California Code of Regulations.
Recent legislation, Chapter 1, Statutes of 2003-04 First Extraordinary Session (ABX1-10, Oropeza), has amended Water Code Section 13260 to remove the maximum limit on an annual fee that the SWRCB can impose and has added Water Code Section 13260.2 requiring the SWRCB to establish a fee for “no exposure” certifications issued to facilities that apply for those certifications in accordance with the general industrial storm water permit.
All fee levels must be increased to generate the fee revenue appropriated in the State Budget Act.
All fees are based on the overall cost of the services provided by the program funded by fees.  ( HYPERLINK "0930-08att_a.doc" Attachment A, attached to this resolution, sets forth the approximate breakdown of the expenditure of money for preparing, implementing, and enforcing a typical individual permit in each of the primary program areas.)
SWRCB staff prepared recommended changes to the fee schedule contained in current regulation to implement the State Budget Act requirements, with input from a working group composed of representatives of fee payer groups that are affected by the increases.
The SWRCB heard comments on the staff proposal at public workshops conducted on July 28, 2003 in northern California and August 11, 2003 in southern California and received 103 comments in writing from interested persons.
The revised terms of Water Code Sections 13260 and the provisions of Water Code Section 13260.2 will not take effect until October 28, 2003; these emergency regulations are being adopted to take effect on that date and not before. The revised fee schedule will be applied to all fees due and owing for fiscal year 2003-2004.
THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED THAT:

SWRCB:

Adopts emergency regulations approving the revisions to Title 23, Division 3, Chapter 9, Article 1, Section 2200 and adding Section 2200.3 of the California Code of Regulations, as recommended by staff, to implement the provisions of the 2003 State Budget Act and pursuant to the provisions of Water Code Section 13260(f)(2) ( HYPERLINK "0930-08att_b.doc" Attachment B).
